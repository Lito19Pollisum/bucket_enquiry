Privacy notice

Pollisum Fabrication Pte Ltd, enquiry form

What we collect

The details you enter on the enquiry form: company name, contact person, phone number, email address (if you give one), project name, delivery location, required date, the items you ask for, and any remarks. Your reference number is generated automatically.

Why we collect it

To prepare your quotation, to contact you about your enquiry, and to follow up with you on it. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

Our sales and design staff who handle enquiries. The form is hosted and stored with technology providers who process it on our behalf, and your enquiry is emailed to our team. If you give an email address, we also send you a confirmation.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and for our business and quotation records.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent to us using them, by contacting us at fabrication@pollisum.com or (65) 6755 7600 and quoting your reference number.
